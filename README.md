# Pain-PD
Cumulative Pain Burden, Plasma Proteomic Signatures, and Incident Parkinson Disease

Data availability
UK Biobank

  UK Biobank data are available to approved researchers through the UK Biobank Research Analysis Platform or Access Management System.

Independent Chinese cohort

  Data from the Chinese Health and Retirement Longitudinal Study are available to registered users through its standard application and data access procedures.

PPMI

  PPMI-derived proteomic association results used for cross-study comparison should be obtained from the original publication or associated repository, subject to the applicable terms of use.
  
  No participant-level data are redistributed in this repository.

Core analytical tools may include:

  R: survival, glmnet, data.table, dplyr, ggplot2, and packages used for restricted cubic splines, mediation, and NRI
  Python: lightgbm, scikit-learn, pandas, and numpy
